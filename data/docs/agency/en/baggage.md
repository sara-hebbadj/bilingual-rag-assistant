# Baggage

## 1. Your baggage allowance

The airline and your fare type decide how much baggage you can take, not Sarab Travel. Your allowance is printed on your e-ticket under "Baggage". Lite (basic) fares often include a cabin bag only, with no checked bag.

## 2. Buying extra baggage

We can add extra checked baggage for you up to 48 hours before departure. The price is set by the airline, and buying it in advance is usually cheaper than paying at the airport. Our service fee is AED 25 per request. Less than 48 hours before departure, please buy extra baggage directly from the airline.

## 3. Sports equipment and musical instruments

Golf bags, bicycles, diving equipment and large musical instruments must be requested at least 72 hours before departure, because the airline has to approve them. The airline may charge a special-item fee. Fragile instruments can sometimes travel in the cabin on a purchased extra seat.

## 4. Lost, delayed or damaged baggage

If your bag is missing or damaged, report it at the airline's baggage desk at the arrival airport before you leave the airport. Ask for a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) and keep its reference number. The airline is responsible for tracing the bag and for any compensation. Send us a copy of the PIR and we will help you follow up. Airlines usually require a written claim within 7 days for damaged baggage and within 21 days for delayed baggage.

## 5. Items you cannot pack in checked baggage

Power banks and spare lithium batteries must travel in your cabin bag, never in checked baggage. Liquids in the cabin are limited by airport security rules. Each airline publishes its own full list of restricted items, so check it before you pack.
