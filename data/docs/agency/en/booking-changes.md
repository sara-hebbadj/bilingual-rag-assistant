# Changing a booking

## 1. What you can change

You can ask us to change the travel date or time, the route, the spelling of a passenger name, or the dates of a hotel stay. Every change depends on two sets of rules: the rules of the airline or hotel (the supplier), and the Sarab Travel service fee described below. If the new option costs more, you pay the fare difference. If it costs less, the airline or hotel decides whether any difference is returned; many fares do not return it.

## 2. Changing a flight date or time

Send your change request at least 24 hours before the original departure time. Our service fee is AED 75 per passenger for each change. On top of this fee, the airline may charge its own change penalty and the fare difference. Lite (basic) fares usually do not allow any changes at all; check the fare rules on your e-ticket. If your flight leaves in less than 24 hours, call our 24/7 urgent travel line, but we cannot guarantee that a change is still possible.

## 3. Correcting a passenger name

The name on the ticket must match the passport exactly. We correct small spelling mistakes of up to three letters without our service fee, although some airlines charge their own fee for corrections. A ticket cannot be transferred to a different person. If someone else needs to travel, the original ticket must be cancelled under the cancellation rules and a new booking made.

## 4. Changing a hotel booking

You can move your hotel dates free of charge until the free-change deadline printed on your hotel voucher, which is usually 48 hours before check-in. After that deadline, a date change is treated as a cancellation followed by a new booking. Room upgrades depend on availability, and you pay the price difference.

## 5. Changing a holiday package

Holiday packages combine flights and hotels. Changes to a package cost AED 150 per booking for each amendment, plus any supplier charges, and must be requested more than 21 days before departure. From 21 days before departure, a change is only possible by cancelling the package under the package cancellation charges and booking again.

## 6. How to request a change

Open My Trips on our website, choose the booking, then select Manage booking and Request a change. During opening hours we reply within 4 working hours. A change is confirmed only when you receive an updated e-ticket or voucher by email. Please do not travel on a change that has not been confirmed in writing.
