# Travel insurance

## 1. Our insurance plans

We sell travel insurance from our partner insurer. There are three plans: Essential, Plus and Annual Multi-Trip. You can add insurance when you book or at any time before you travel.

## 2. What each plan covers

- Essential: emergency medical costs up to USD 50,000, trip cancellation up to AED 5,000, and delayed baggage up to AED 500.
- Plus: emergency medical costs up to USD 250,000, trip cancellation up to AED 15,000, delayed baggage up to AED 1,500, and visa refusal cover.
- Annual Multi-Trip: Plus-level cover for an unlimited number of trips in 12 months, with a maximum of 45 days per trip.

## 3. Prices and age limits

Essential starts at AED 49 per trip, Plus at AED 99 per trip, and Annual Multi-Trip at AED 599 per year. These prices are for travellers aged 18 to 64. Travellers aged 65 to 79 pay a higher premium, and travellers aged 80 or over cannot buy insurance online and should contact our team.

## 4. Cooling-off period

You can cancel your insurance within 14 days of buying it and get the full premium back, as long as the trip has not started and you have not made a claim. After 14 days, the premium is not refundable.

## 5. Making a claim

Claims are made directly to the insurer, not to Sarab Travel, within 30 days of your return. Keep all receipts, medical reports and, for baggage problems, the airline's Property Irregularity Report (PIR). The 24/7 emergency assistance number is printed on your insurance certificate.

## 6. What is not covered

The plans do not cover pre-existing medical conditions unless you declared them and the insurer accepted them. Extreme sports are only covered with the Adventure add-on, which costs AED 60 per trip. Travel to a destination against official government travel advice is not covered.
