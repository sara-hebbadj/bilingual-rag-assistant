# Refunds

## 1. How refunds work

After a cancellation is confirmed, we ask the airline or hotel to return the refundable amount. We then pass on to you what the supplier returns, minus any Sarab Travel fees that apply. We cannot refund money that the supplier has not yet returned to us.

## 2. How long a refund takes

Airlines can take up to 60 days to return money to us, and sometimes up to 90 days. Once we receive the money, a refund to a card reaches your account within 7 to 14 working days. A refund by bank transfer takes up to 10 working days. In total, most refunds arrive between 2 and 8 weeks after the cancellation.

## 3. Where the money goes

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If you paid in cash at our office, we refund by bank transfer to an account in the traveller's own name, and we need the IBAN. We cannot send a refund to a card or account that belongs to somebody else.

## 4. Travel Credit instead of money

Instead of a cash refund, you can choose Travel Credit worth 110% of the refundable amount. Travel Credit is valid for 12 months, can be used for any Sarab Travel booking, and cannot be transferred to another person or exchanged for cash later.

## 5. What is never refunded

Some charges cannot be refunded in any case: our service fees, visa assistance fees, the card surcharge on American Express payments, and travel insurance premiums once the insurance cooling-off period has ended.

## 6. Checking a refund or reporting a delay

You can follow the status of a refund in My Trips. If you have not received your refund 60 days after the cancellation, email our refunds team at refunds@example.com with your booking reference and your CX- cancellation number. We will chase the supplier and update you within 3 working days.
