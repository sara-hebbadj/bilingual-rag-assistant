# Payments

## 1. Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, and Apple Pay. Bank transfer is accepted only for bookings made more than 7 days before travel. You can also pay cash in UAE dirhams (AED) at our Dubai office.

## 2. Currency and card charges

All prices are shown and charged in AED. If your card was issued outside the UAE, your bank may add a foreign transaction fee; this fee comes from your bank, not from us. American Express payments carry a 2% card surcharge. There is no surcharge for Visa, Mastercard or Apple Pay.

## 3. Paying in instalments

For holiday packages above AED 3,000, you can pay in 3 monthly instalments through our instalment partner, with 0% interest when every instalment is paid on time. Instalments are only available when departure is at least 60 days away, and the final instalment must be paid 14 days before departure.

## 4. Deposits for holiday packages

To hold a holiday package, you pay a deposit of 25% of the package price. The balance is due 30 days before departure. If the balance is not paid on time, we may cancel the booking, and the package cancellation charges apply. Some packages have their own payment terms, which are stated in their own terms and replace these general rules.

## 5. Safe payments

We only send payment links from our own website domain. Our staff will never ask for your card PIN or a one-time password (OTP) by phone, email or WhatsApp. If you receive a suspicious message that uses our name, forward it to security@example.com and do not pay.

## 6. Invoices and VAT

We email a tax invoice after full payment. UAE VAT of 5% is charged on our service fees. If your company needs an invoice with its name and tax registration number (TRN), tell us before you pay, because we cannot reissue invoices afterwards.
