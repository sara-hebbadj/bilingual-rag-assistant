# Cancelling a booking

## 1. How to cancel

You can cancel from My Trips on our website, by email or by phone. A cancellation is only complete when we send you a cancellation reference number that starts with CX-. Keep this number: you will need it if you ask about a refund later.

## 2. Cancelling a flight

What you get back depends on the fare you bought. For refundable fares, the airline deducts its cancellation penalty and we charge a cancellation service fee of AED 100 per passenger. For non-refundable fares, usually only some airport taxes can be returned, and only if the airline allows it. If you do not cancel before departure and do not take the flight (a no-show), the ticket can lose its whole value apart from some taxes.

## 3. Cancelling a hotel

Hotel bookings can be cancelled free of charge until the cancellation deadline shown on your voucher. After the deadline, the hotel normally charges the first night, unless the voucher states a different charge. Rooms booked at a non-refundable rate cannot be refunded at any time.

## 4. Cancelling a holiday package

Package cancellation charges are a percentage of the total package price and depend on how long before departure you cancel:

- 45 days or more before departure: 10%
- 44 to 21 days before departure: 25%
- 20 to 8 days before departure: 50%
- 7 days or less before departure, or no-show: 100%

## 5. The 24-hour free cancellation window

If you booked a flight on our website, you can cancel it within 24 hours of making the booking without paying our cancellation service fee, as long as the flight departs more than 7 days later. The airline's own fare rules still apply during this window.

## 6. When the airline or hotel cancels

If an airline cancels your flight or moves it by more than 3 hours, you can choose either an alternative flight with no Sarab Travel fees or a full refund of the unused part of the booking. If a hotel cannot honour your booking, we offer a similar hotel at no extra cost or a full refund for that hotel. We tell you about these changes by email and SMS.
