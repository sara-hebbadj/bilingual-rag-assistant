# Contact and support

## 1. Opening hours

Our customer care team works Monday to Saturday from 9:00 to 21:00, and Sunday from 10:00 to 18:00, Gulf Standard Time (GST). Travellers who depart within the next 24 hours can call our urgent travel line at any time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## 2. How to reach us

- Live chat on our website, answered straight away during opening hours.
- Email: support@example.com, answered within 1 working day.
- Phone: +971 50 000 0100.
- WhatsApp: +971 50 000 0101.
- Urgent travel line (24/7): +971 50 000 0199.

## 3. Complaints and escalation

If you are not happy with how your case was handled, reply to your case email with the word "Escalate". A team leader will contact you within 2 working days. If the issue is still not solved, our complaints manager will review it within 5 working days. You will receive a final written response within 15 working days of your first complaint.

## 4. Languages

Our team supports customers in Arabic, English and French. Tell us which language you prefer when you contact us.

## 5. What the online assistant can and cannot do

The online assistant answers general questions using these help pages only, and shows the page it used for each answer. It cannot see, change or cancel your bookings, and it cannot take payments. For anything about a specific booking, contact a human agent through one of the channels above.
