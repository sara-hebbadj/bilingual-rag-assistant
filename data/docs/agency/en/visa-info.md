# Visas: general information

## 1. Important: this is general information only

This page is general information, not legal or immigration advice. Entry and visa rules are set by governments and can change at any time. The traveller is responsible for holding a valid passport, visa and any other travel documents. Before you book, always confirm the current requirements with the embassy or consulate of your destination, or on its official government website. Sarab Travel cannot tell you whether a visa will be granted.

## 2. What our Visa Assistance service does

For a fee of AED 120 per application, plus the government and visa-centre fees charged at cost, we check that your application form is complete, book an appointment at the visa centre where one is needed, and prepare supporting documents such as a flight reservation and a hotel confirmation letter. We do not guarantee that a visa will be approved. Our fee and the government fees are not refunded if a visa is refused.

## 3. Documents that are often requested

The exact list depends on the destination, but many countries ask for:

- a passport valid for at least 6 months after your return date, with at least 2 blank pages;
- recent passport-size photos;
- bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months;
- a letter from your employer and, for UAE residents, a copy of your residence visa;
- travel insurance that covers the whole trip.

## 4. When to apply

Apply at least 4 weeks before you travel, and at least 8 weeks before travel in the peak seasons of June to August and December. Processing times are decided by the authorities, and we cannot speed them up.

## 5. If your visa is refused

If a visa is refused, the normal cancellation rules of your flights, hotels and packages still apply. Until your visa is issued, we recommend refundable fares and rates, or travel insurance that includes visa refusal cover.
